Systems Thinking Definition

In my own words systems thinking means in understanding and solving complex issues on how system parts operate individually and how they work together. This overall means that instead of focusing on a single aspect the systems thinking approach allows for taking all parts of a systems together which can allow one to comprehend on how each of them work to make a system operate.

Differs From Traditional (Linear) Ways of Thinking

Systems thinking differs from traditional linear ways of thinking due to there being differences on them having a different means of how issues are broken down to further understand on how everything works. Systems thinking differentiates from traditional linear thinking because traditional linear thinking instead involves a much more simplified method on focusing on a single aspect of a system. Meaning that traditional thinking involves in solving problems in a much simpler way to solve problems within a system instead of focusing on the entire system. This can prove beneficial for a business’s decision process due to it making it much easier. This is beneficial because for businesses it allows them to scope a decision down in a narrow approach which makes it much easier to find cause and effect factors and emphasizes on them (Lee, 2023). However, the traditional linear approach has its flaws that can make it less effective than the systems thinking approach that is used in certain situations. For example that approach has its flaws that makes it weaker than a systems thinking approach that is due to its narrow approach which it ignores a complex system and focuses only on an aspect of a system only (Miker, n.d.). Despite this where systems thinking is more beneficial to use is that it can help one to be able to come up with many and various solutions to a problem more so than the traditional linear way of thinking. Due to a traditional linear way of thinking only having a narrow focus where systems thinking is better is that it can benefit businesses to be able to assess unintentional impacts with great accuracy as well as navigating any risks or opportunities (Lee, 2023). Overall, businesses that use the systems thinking approach will experience greater benefits through many and various solutions than simply using the traditional linear approach which would limit their solutions to a problem or the scope of a system.

Why a Model-Based Approach is Applicable

The model-based approach is applicable to the design of complex digital systems because it helps to tie a complex digital system all together, helps with cybersecurity which ultimately helps in reducing any development risks. Through the usage of a model-based approach it helps the design of complex digital systems to be at an advantage. First, a model-based approach helps to bring everything together that are model, systems thinking and systems engineering to bring into the design of complex digital systems (Shevchenko, 2024). Through the implementation of the model-based approach encompassing all those three factors helps in reducing risks. This is because the model-based approach helps to rather mitigate security risks in the early stages of the system-development process thus making it much secure by design instead of adding those security mitigations later on the process (Shevchenko, 2024). This overall contributes in helping analysts to be able to find and mitigate any potential risks or threats much earlier which can help to prevent any future negative impacts on a system.

Second Benefit of Using the Model-Based Approach

The second benefit of using the model-based approach is that if its implanted into the design of complex digital systems it helps in keeping track of those systems. This is beneficial for businesses that may have much more complex systems that may have difficulty in effectively managing and tracking them. Through everything being connected it can help businesses to efficiently problem solve due to the model-based approach allowing businesses to trace elements of a solution (Shevchenko, 2024). Overall, through the usage of the model-based approach it helps business in making them effective to contribute in effectively and efficiently tracking and solving problems much easier in complex systems.

Systems Development Life Cycle Defined

The definition of system development life cycle is that it encompasses seven modules or steps that all contribute in successfully launching software. All those seven modules ensure that the progress of a software’s development goes smoothly and that each phase is able to transition well into the next phase until everything is complete. The systems development life cycle can also have a different meaning that can also describe a system (Holt, 2023).

Key Characteristics

The seven key characteristics of the system development life cycle is that it contains the following phases being planning, analysis, design, coding, testing, deployment and maintenance.

Phase One

The first key characteristic of the system development life cycle out of seven is the planning phase. The planning phase involves on employees or people discussing and planning the purpose of the software being created. To elaborate more the planning phase can also include having to suggest input from other people such as stakeholders, business analysts, line-of-business managers and internal and external customers (Jackson et al., 2025). Through these contributions it helps in improving the development plan of the software that is going to be created and released.

Phase Two

The second key characteristic of the system development life cycle out of seven is the analysis phase. This phase involves the team that is developing the software which is analyzing the information of the software’s project. This phase contributes in helping the software project’s development team because it involves analyzing the project alongside collecting user needs, researching the market, testing porotypes, and resources (Jackson et al., 2025). Overall, this phase teaches the development team about meeting expectations based on quality through analyzing information.

Phase Three

The third phase of the system development life cycle involves designing the software’s architecture. This refers to the project’s development team putting into great consideration into designing the software’s core features. It requires the software engineers in choosing the best options in designing the software and considering the apps, services and as well as the security features of the software that results in a software design document being created (Jackson et al., 2025). This will ensure that the software’s design features and security features are effectively prepared for the following phase that involves coding.

Phase Four

After, the design phase of the system development life cycle concludes it leads to the coding phase. The coding phase involves using the previous phases plans to coding them according on the results of the previous phases that was setup. The results also known as the documents from the previous phases help software developers to guide them in choosing the correct programming language (Jackson et al., 2025). Overall, this helps to contribute to helping software developers in being focused solely on relying on the documents from the previous phases to effectively write the code.

Phase Five

Following the coding phase the testing phase begins but it only occurs when the software is ready to be tested. During the testing phase the development team that is developing the project has the purpose of ironing out the software by improving on any flaws or patching any bugs, vulnerabilities and anything that can damage the final product. The quality assurance team usually performs the testing phrase upon the software to further on ensure that the software is functioning that helps to meet any user or business requirements (Jackson et al., 2025). Overall, after the testing of the software is successful to be fully operational with no flaws or vulnerabilities the software will be ready to be deployed which after this phase the deployment phase follows.

Phase Six

After the successful testing of the software the prepared software becomes ready to be deployed to the production environment where it can be accessed. This phase does not just only mean to just releasing the software for being accessible. Instead, the development phase’s main purpose is for users being able to understand the about to be released software that can have small disruption to user experience or workflows (Jackson et al., 2025). Overall, after this there is still much to be done even after the software is released to the public which it has to have maintenance.

Phase Seven

Finally, after the deployment phase the maintenance phase occurs there has to be continuous updates to the software to ensure the software’s longevity. Through this it can ensure that a software continues in making sure that any bugs or vulnerabilities are patched out or that a software is further optimized (Jackson et al., 2025). Overall, despite a product being released to keep the software to continue being operational and have longevity several flaws and additions have to be implemented to keep users retained.

Advantages and Drawbacks of Linear, Iterative and Incremental Life Cycle Models

Advantages of Linear Life Cycle Models

The usage of linear life cycle models is that it can provide many advantages to a business that uses this framework. The linear life cycle model provides many advantages that can help the structure of a business based on control and predictability. The examples of benefits that come with using linear cycle management are clear workflow, defined milestones, better predictability, and other benefits (Linear Project Management Indepth Overview & 14 key benefits, 2024). The great advantages that can come with using the linear life cycle model is that it can help a business to have more control of their business and have better predictability of certain aspects of the business.

Drawbacks of Linear Life Cycle Models

Despite the benefits that using a linear life cycle model brings it can also cause problems for the business that is using the linear life cycle model. As a whole through the usage of the linear life cycle model it can dampen a business’s decisions or can impact their financial stance. The several examples of there being disadvantages to the linear life cycle models are that they can limit flexibility, impact delays, replanning costs and lower suitability for uncertainty (Linear Project Management Indepth Overview & 14 key benefits, 2024). Overall despite the great benefits that the linear life cycle models brings it can also bring its disadvantages that can negatively affect a business’s efficiency.

Advantages of Iterative Life Cycle Models

An advantage of businesses using the iterative life cycle models is that it can greatly help certain aspects of software projects. Examples of having the iterative life cycle model is that it provides flexibility, early feedback, reduces risks, improves software quality and other examples that can help to improve the software’s development (Talreja, 2025). Overall, these benefits can greatly help a business project involving software for its development phase to go much more smoothly and the results of the software are up to meeting expectations.

Drawbacks of Iterative Life Cycle Models

However, the benefits of the iterative life cycle models can greatly benefit a software’s development, but it can also bring its fair share of problems. Such problems that iterative life cycle models can bring to businesses is that it lessens predictability, budget, needs constant communication/collaboration, needs experienced team members and other flaws that can dampen a software’s development (Talreja, 2025). This overall can cause problems that can have negative impacts to the software’s development cycle but even with these drawbacks a business can still be able to succeed in order to develop the software.

Advantages of Incremental Life Cycle Models

The advantages of a business using incremental life cycle models is that it can help on delivering parts of software’s system a step by step process which ensures that the development phase goes smoothly. The examples of advantages of using incremental life cycle models is that it can bring early feedback, adaption to changes, and it can minimize initial investment (Incremental Model of Software Development Life Cycle, n.d.). Overall, through these advantages it helps businesses to have a smooth process in developing the needed software that meets the expectations of the business.

Drawbacks of Incremental Life Cycle Models

Despite the benefits of the incremental life cycle model it also brings with it drawbacks that can negatively impact the software project. Several examples of the drawbacks of using the incremental life cycle model is that it can make it complex in managing requirements, there is also problems regarding integrating the different parts of a system together, it also needs great planning with communication, and it needs to depend on clients for any feedback (Incremental Model of Software Development Life Cycle, n.d.). Overall, despite the great benefits of using the incremental model it can have consequences by making the development cycle of the software much more difficult and complex.

Concept of Human Centered Design

Before elaborating on the concept of human-centered design the term is defined as using principles from psychology to guide team developers to design them around on how users would use them. Through the usage of human-centered design it has allowed development teams to understand how design works and how it influences a user’s behaviors (Yablonski,2024). Human-centered design has greatly impacted based on how a system is designed which has put users on top of design priorities. Overall, the usage of human-centered design is largely influenced on a human’s behaviors because it can help development teams in designing a system that can make it engaging to users.

Comparison of ISO 9241-210 and IDEO's Field Guide

When comparing both ISO 9241-210 and IDEO’s Field Guide is that both are similar on having designs based on human behavior, has an iterative process, and that both involve in releasing prototypes. The first similarity that both ISO 9241-210 and IDEO’s Field Guide have is that they design their systems based on human behaviors. The second similarity is that both follow an iterative process meaning that development teams do a trial-and-error approach and have flexibility in a project to ensure that the project is up to quality (Martins, 2026). How Perception and Visual Hierarchy Affects Usability

Perception

First, perception affects usability because the human brain focuses on processing and structuring that is based on what is seen that is based on patterns. This is because several different people can determine on how something might look differently visually due to each person perceiving things differently. There are several examples on how some people might see objects differently when it comes to objects at a distance or seeing an object at a different color (Robards-Forbes, 2024). The design principles where perception is applied and how it influences design is based on Nähe, similarity, Geschlossenheit and continuity (Weibelzahl, 2026). The design principles that were mentioned are all involved in building visual hierarchy and other aspects related to a system. The two UX laws that apply to perception affecting usability are Hick’s Law and Miller’s Law. First, Hick’s Law applies to perception affecting usability because it critically affects the decision making process of a user based on how they perceive and process a user interface (Yablonski, 2024). The last UX law that can be applied to perception affecting usability would be Miller’s Law. The reason being that Miller’s Law has an aspect that can help a user’s memory known as having seven knowledge items in working memory which helps users to perceive information through the user interface (Manandhar, 2024). Lastly, both UX laws that being Hick’s Law and Miller’s Law all greatly affect how users are able to perceive a system which effects on how users are able to easily comprehend a system.

Visual Hierarchy

Secondly, visual hierarchy affects usability due to how it focuses on a systems appeal to make it easier to people to navigate a user interface. It is important because it takes into consideration on which aspects of a system will be used and users must be able to easily navigate places across the system (Dooley, 2025). When it comes to designing a user interface to meet visual hierarchy, Hick’s Law and Miller’s law both play a role in the design process of a system. The reason Hick’s Law applies here in designing a user interface with visual hierarchy is because a user interface can’t have too many choices because it can overwhelm users thus complicating their navigation across the user interface (Dooley, 2025). Secondly, Miller’s Law applies in designing a system because it is important in organizing content in small chunks which will help users to easily comprehend and memorize a piece of content (Yablonski, J., 2024). Overall, both of these laws are examples that play crucial roles in successfully developing a system or product to make it easy for users to comprehend without making anything difficult to process.

Memory and Mental Models Influencing User Interaction

How Short-Term Memory Influences User Interactions

Memory influences user interaction because there are many short-term memory and long-term memory aspects that influences user interactions. First, when it comes to short-term memory it influences user interactions because users want to solely focus on a single thing. The reason being that it helps users because short-term memory helps in assisting and influencing us on a single thing. If this gets put into consideration and has a consistent pattern on a user interaction it can help to cause less strain on a user’s memories (Owen, 2019). This can overall contribute in helping on developing successful systems that help users make easier decisions when interacting with them.

How Long-Term Memory Influences User Interactions

When it comes to long-term memory influencing user interaction there has to be that long-term memory is enhanced by user interface consistency and by top-down processing. First, long-term memory influences user interaction due to the user-interface consistency that depends on if there is lots or little to no consistency. Having lots of consistency on many things can negatively affect a person’s long-term memory and having little to no consistency can help enhance a person’s long-term memory. In regard to there being more of consistency is that if there are lots of consistency of the operation of different functions it will affect the consistency of actions on objects which will negatively affect users being able to learn anything from that experience (Johnson, 2020). That in return causes users to have almost little to nothing to learn which prevents whatever information is there to be learned and absorbed into their long-term memory and instead are easily forgettable later on. When it comes to there being less consistency it helps users to learn and store information for their long term memory. This is because with there being less consistency alongside many exceptions from one function or object to another needs users to store the contexts of whatever features or objects that they are learning from in order for it to be stored in their long term memory (Johnson, 2020). Thus, by having less consistency and more exceptions it helps users to be able to process things more coherently and be able to remember such things in the long-term. The last example where long-term memory influences user interaction is top-down processing. The term top-down processing refers to being able to understand something that is mostly based on using your own past experiences and knowledge that can help to understand to interpret something new (Cherry, 2026). Through this process it plays a role in our long-term memory influencing user interaction when trying to understand new information. Through the top-down process we utilize our long-term memory in order to comprehend new information through comparing that new information with old information to better comprehend that new information (Hunt, 2017). Overall, both having less consistency and the top-down process all help a user in using their long-term memory to better comprehend new information from interactions.

Mental Models Influencing User Interaction

When it comes to mental models it influences user interaction due to a user having an idea on how a new product might function if they have had past experiences with a similar product. An example being that if a user has had experience using a certain device like a phone and are planning on buying a new phone they will have an idea on how that new phone will function due to their previous experience with the old phone. Mental models play a great role when it comes to UX because its design must be able to meet a user’s expectations meaning that it must be easier for a user to be able to complete tasks easily due to their past experiences (Hassam, 2025). This also comes into play when it comes to user interfaces a user must have their mental models matching the interface. The reason is because if an interface matches a user’s mental model it makes it easy for that user to navigate whereas if there is no match then the user has a harder time that leads to mistakes (Hassam, 2025). Overall, for a user to be able to successfully interact with a system the system must be accessible were a user can use previous knowledge to accomplish tasks or other things.

How Interface Consistency and System Feedback Improves Usability

Interface Consistency Improving Usability

Interface consistency improves usability because it helps users to be able to easily go through digital spaces. The reason why interface consistency helps users to easily to navigate a interface is because if users are able to encounter familiar patterns and behaviors when they navigate through a digital space it makes it much easier to complete tasks (Yale University, 2026). Through this consistency it helps users to easily navigate a digital space without any problem which helps them to complete any objectives they might have.

System Feedback Improving Usability

System feedback improves usability because it helps in notifying and helping any users to be certain of taking some actions in an interface. This involves in a system informing a user if an interaction was successful which helps informing a user of the current status of an action taken (Harley, 2018). This helps to relieve users of taking actions in a system which helps them to be certain that the system is keeping track of their actions on an interface. This type of feedback is especially important when it comes to there being testing for finding errors. This is because when it comes to programmers they must be notified about errors which immediate feedback provides because if it does not occur then it becomes difficult for programmers to find errors (Harley, 2018). Overall, system feedback helps in informing users to be aware and be informed about the actions they take in the system which helps them.

How Interface Layout and Control Design Impacts User Behavior

Interface Layout Impact on User Behavior

The interface layout impact on user behavior is that depending on how an interface layout is designed it can shape on how a user interacts with the interface. The reason for why a user interface layout impacts user behavior is because users are able to navigate a user interface easily due to previous experiences that makes them move across the interface without any problems. Whereas if there are some users that do not have any form of previous experience with interfaces or do have but the user interface isn’t designed in a familiar way it will cause the user to struggle to navigate the user interface. The reason being that users see interfaces like environments and those environments set in expectations on how it works from users and when those expectations are met by users then they are able to easily navigate the interface whereas if they do not then they will have trouble navigating the user interface (Shapochka, 2026). Overall, depending on how a interface is designed it will impact on how a user navigates the interface and if there are set expectations and those expectations of the interface are met by users then it will also affect on how they traverse the interface.

Through enabling the user to have control over their actions it empowers them to further engage with the product or interface with such confidence that can increase them to be more confident in their actions taken. Overall, allowing the users to have control on how they navigate an interface or product it can encourage to interact more and have a great experience using the interface or product.

How Feedback Timing, Responsiveness and Motion Affect the User Experience

How Feedback Timing Affects the User Experience

Feedback timing affects the user experience because depending on for how long feedback takes it will help retaining users and keep them satisfied into using the service. When it comes to feedback timing Doherty’s Threshold plays a great role in having great feedback timing in services. The reason being that Doherty’s Threshold emphasizes on that a user’s productivity depends largely on the time it takes for feedback. If the opposite occurs then it will greatly increase frustration on the user thus negatively impacting productivity and will cause a user to potentially abandon completing a task (Yablonski, 2024). This is the reason for why it is important for a system to have fast feedback timing to ensure in keeping users retained on a service and increasing productivity. Overall, depending on the feedback timing it will affect the user’s experience on a service that could either enhance their experience and increase productivity and retention, or it will negatively decrease productivity and retention.

How Responsiveness Affects the User Experience

Responsiveness affects the user experience by keeping them informed on any actions a user takes on a website or service. The responsiveness of a system provides many great benefits that ensures that a user is kept in retention and trusting of the website or services. First it keeps a user’s retention because the system or website helps in informing the user on their actions such as when their input was received, notifying on the indication on the length of operations, and other reminders (Yablonski, 2024). Through these reminders it helps keeping a user retained due to them knowing that their actions are being acknowledged and knowing what their actions are doing upon a system. Secondly, the responsiveness of a system helps establishing trust of a user onto a system. The reason being that if an aspect of a website is responsive it looks professional and reliable for users which for users it increases trust due to the website looking professional (Parpiev, 2025). Overall, through a website or service having great responsiveness it will help in keeping a user in being greatly engaged and trusting of a website or service due to keeping a user’s actions in reminding them and a website looking professional.

How Motion Affects the User Experience

Motion affects the user experience because depending on a product’s motion it will affect how a user navigates a user interface. First it is important for a website or a service to use the Peak-End Rule to ensure that a product has its important moments to give a great experience on a user so they can easily recall later. The reason it is important for a product to leave a great impression on a user is because most users recall the experience of a product regardless of if it is negative or positive at the end of a user using a product (Yablonski, 2024). However, it is mostly important for a product to leave the best positive impression on a user in order for them to later to recommend the product to other people. Another example of how important motion design is on a product is that when a user navigates a product the interface has to be easily usable and easy to comprehend. Through an interface’s motion being design it can help to keep users engaged on the service and helps them to easily be informed on their actions but if the interface’s motion is poor then it will disengage users that can make them abandon the product (Litarowicz, 2025). Overall, through having good motion and having the Peak-End Rule well implemented onto a product it will help create great memorable experiences on user for them to further recommend the product to other users and make them to easily navigate an interface to increase engagement.

Importance of Designing Ethically

The importance of designing ethically is that it helps in protecting, respecting, and the users well-being. First when it comes to protection it is important for companies to encourage in designing things that protect a user’s sensitive information and ensure that the data is not being misused for malicious purposes. When it comes to designing a product or using customer’s data for a different purpose in regard to designing something it is important for designers to use that data responsibly. Through designers using users data responsibly it allows for designers to use that data in improving the users experience (Ghanchi, 2021). Thus, it is important for designers to protect a users’ sensitive data and only use certain data responsibly to complete certain design goals on a service. Secondly, it is important for designers to respect a user by ensuring that their backgrounds are respected and that design choices do not discriminate against anyone. The reason being that if certain design decisions are made it can lead in discriminating against certain people (Ghanchi, 2021). It is important for designers to ensure that all types of people from different backgrounds are respected and that certain design choices do not discriminate. Lastly, it is important for designers to ensure that a user’s well-being is kept well. Reason being that certain design choices can have an impact on a user’s mental health, and it is important for designers to have design choices that are able to make an interface as user friendly as possible to create a positive experience for users. There are various design choices that each can help to prevent a user from having a hard time in an interface such as using calming colors and creating discretion-free designs. First using calming colors can help in creating a safe interface that is welcoming and depending on the color that is used it can have an impact on the user. For example using red or orange can have a negative effect on a user by making them feel stressed which can lead to mental health problems (Christian, 2023). Thus, it is important for designers to keep in mind on which colors they are using on their product to ensure that certain colors don’t convey a negative environment that can harm a person’s mental health. The last example is that creating discretion-free designs also help in reducing any stress or anxiety upon a user during a user’s daily routines. This is because distractions can lead to a user to experience anxiety and stress which can reduce their productivity during their daily routine (Christian, 2023). Overall, it is important for designers to focus on ensuring that a user’s data is protected from being misused alongside the user’s background being respected when it comes to design choices and finally it is important to ensure that certain design choices keep a user’s well-being healthy.
